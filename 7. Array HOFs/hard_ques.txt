Q1. Employee Salary Analyzer~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
let employees = [
  {name: "A", salary: 25000, department: "IT"},
  {name: "B", salary: 40000, department: "HR"},
  {name: "C", salary: 60000, department: "IT"},
  {name: "D", salary: 35000, department: "IT"},
  {name: "E", salary: 70000, department: "HR"}
];

Return an object containing:
- Names of IT employees.
- IT employees earning more than ₹30,000.
- Average salary of IT employees.
- Highest salary among IT employees.




Q2. Nested HOF Challenge~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Given:
let students = [
  {name: "A", marks: [80, 90, 70]},
  {name: "B", marks: [60, 55, 65]},
  {name: "C", marks: [95, 90, 100]},
  {name: "D", marks: [40, 50, 45]}
];

Return names of students whose average marks are at least 80.
Use HOFs. No traditional loops.

Q5. Ultimate HOF Challenge~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Given:
let students = [
  {name: "Aman", marks: 72},
  {name: "Kartik", marks: 91},
  {name: "Rohit", marks: 85},
  {name: "Rahul", marks: 64},
  {name: "Arjun", marks: 95}
];

Create analyzeStudents(students) returning:
- Names of students scoring 80 or more.
- Their total marks.
- Their average marks.
- Highest scorer's name.
- Whether any student scored 95 or more.
- Count of students who failed (marks below 40).
Handle an empty array and cases where no student qualifies.
