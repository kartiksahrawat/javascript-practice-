Conditional statement questions
💻 Question 1 — Electricity Bill

Take the number of units from the user.

Billing:

0–100 → ₹5/unit
101–200 → ₹7/unit
201–500 → ₹10/unit
Above 500 → ₹15/unit

If the bill is more than ₹3000, give a 10% discount.

Print:

Unit Rate
Total Bill
Discount (if applicable)
Final Bill
