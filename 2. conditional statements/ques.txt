Q4. Smallest of Three Numbers
Take three numbers from the user and find the smallest number.

Q5. Leap Year
Take a year from the user and check whether it is a leap year or not.

Q6. Voting Eligibility
Take age from the user and check whether the person is eligible to vote (age >= 18).

Q7. Divisibility by 3 and 5
Take a number and check whether it is divisible by both 3 and 5, only 3, only 5, or neither.

Q8. Grade Calculator
Take marks (0–100) and assign a grade: 90–100 A, 80–89 B, 70–79 C, 60–69 D, below 60 F.

Q9. Exam Eligibility
Take attendance (%) and fee status. Student is eligible only if attendance >= 75% and fees are paid.

Q10. Login System
Take username and password. Correct username = "admin" and password = "12345". Check whether login is successful or not.

Q11. Shopping Discount
Take purchase amount. Give 20% discount for >= ₹5000, 15% for >= ₹3000, 10% for >= ₹1000, otherwise no discount. Print discount and final amount.

Q12. Age Category
Take age and classify it as Child (0–12), Teenager (13–19), Adult (20–59), or Senior Citizen (60+).

Q13. Valid Triangle
Take three side lengths and check whether they can form a valid triangle.

Q14. Triangle Type
Take three sides and determine whether the triangle is Equilateral, Isosceles, or Scalene. First check that the triangle is valid.

Q15. Vowel or Consonant
Take one character and check whether it is a vowel or consonant. Handle invalid characters too.

Q16. Second Largest of Three
Take three numbers and find the second largest number. Handle equal numbers appropriately.

Q17. Number in Range
Take a number, lower limit, and upper limit. Check whether the number lies between the limits (inclusive).

Q18. Password Validation
Take a password and check whether it has at least 8 characters, one uppercase, one lowercase, and one digit.

Q19. Exam Eligibility
Take age, marks, and attendance. Student is eligible if age >= 18, marks >= 60, and attendance >= 75%.

Q20. Employee Bonus
Take salary and years of experience. Bonus = 20% for >=10 years, 10% for >=5 years, otherwise 5%. Print bonus and final salary.

Q21. Loan Eligibility
Take age, monthly salary, and employment status. Eligible if age is 21–60, salary >= ₹30,000, and status is "employed".

Q22. Income Tax
Take annual income. Tax = 0% for <= ₹2.5L, 5% for ₹2.5L–₹5L, 20% for ₹5L–₹10L, and 30% above ₹10L.

Q23. ATM Withdrawal
Take PIN, balance, and withdrawal amount. Allow withdrawal only if PIN = 1234, amount > 0, and amount <= balance. Print remaining balance.

Q24. Multiple of 7 or 11
Take a number and check whether it is a multiple of both 7 and 11, only 7, only 11, or neither.

Q25. Shipping Charges
Take order amount and membership status. Shipping is free if amount >= ₹2000 OR membership is "Premium"; otherwise shipping = ₹100. Print final amount.

Q26. Greatest Among Four
Take four numbers and find the greatest number using conditional/nested conditions.

Q27. Valid Date
Take day, month, and year and check whether the date is valid. Consider month days and leap year February (28/29 days).

Q28. Movie Ticket Price
Take age and day. Price: below 12 = ₹100, 12–59 = ₹200, 60+ = ₹120. Add ₹50 on Saturday/Sunday.

Q29. Employee Promotion
Take performance rating, attendance, and experience. Employee qualifies if rating >= 4, attendance >= 90%, and experience >= 3 years.
